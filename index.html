How It Works

Simple by design

EvaluRate gets out of your way so you can focus on teaching.

Open EvaluRate

Launch the app on your iPhone before the evaluation begins. No sign-in or configuration needed.

Start Monitoring

Tap to begin. EvaluRate immediately starts detecting and timing each compression.

Read the Rate

Watch the live BPM readout and see at a glance whether the student is on target, too fast, or too slow.

Review & Debrief

When the session ends, review the session summary to support meaningful feedback.
